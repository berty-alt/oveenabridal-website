# Product Plan

## Customer journey

1. Discover Oveena and browse the collection.
2. Review a dress, sizes, price, media, and provisional availability.
3. Request a fitting and provide contact details.
4. Submit a rental request and accept the approved rental terms.
5. Wait for administrator review.
6. Pay by cash or bank transfer when instructed.
7. Attend fittings, collect the dress at the shop, and return it to the shop.
8. View booking and payment status from the customer account in a later phase.

## Administrator journey

1. Maintain dresses, variants, media, prices, and blocks.
2. Review appointments and rental requests.
3. Recheck dates and approve, reject, or propose changes.
4. Record cash or verified bank-transfer payments.
5. Manage fittings, preparation, pickup, return, inspection, and closure.
6. Review audit history for sensitive changes.

## First implementation milestone

Deliver a responsive English public website with the Oveena brand, real contact details, collection-ready seed content, and clear fitting/rental-request calls to action. It must be accessible, fast, and structured for later database-backed functionality.

## Owner content still needed

- Rental periods and buffer days
- Deposit and balance rules

- Damage, late-return, and cleaning charges
- Bank-transfer instructions
- Dress catalogue names, codes, categories, sizes, prices, and images
- Social profile URLs
- Confirmation of whether the supplied email and daily opening hours are current
